Heavy Tonnage Capacity

SUPER-HEAVY STRUCTURAL STEEL FABRICATION, HIGHWAY BRIDGES & INDUSTRIAL COMPONENTS

SteelCraft is an AISC-certified Advanced Major Steel Bridge Fabricator capable of handling super-heavy structural components weighing up to 250 metric tons per assembly. We manufacture highway bridge spans, plate girders, heavy mill transfer bents, offshore marine modules, and rotary industrial equipment.

Our 200,000 sq ft manufacturing facility features 40-foot hook heights, twin 65-metric-ton overhead bridge cranes (130 MT tandem capacity), and private rail spur logistics connected directly to deepwater Gulf port facilities for oversized transshipment.

Core Technical Capabilities

Highway & Railroad Bridge Girders

Fabrication of continuous plate girders, box girders, and fracture-critical bridge members certified to AASHTO / AWS D1.5.

Heavy Overhead Crane Runways

High-cycle fatigue-resistant runway girders and surge truss systems for 50-ton to 250-ton steel mill cranes.

Heavy Industrial Vessels & Kilns

Rotary kiln shells, heavy ductwork, blast furnace shells, and heavy rolled plate assemblies up to 100mm thickness.

Heavy Modular Pre-Assemblies

Complete trial-shop assembly of oversized structural bents ensuring guaranteed zero-mismatch field erection.

Fabrication Work Process

Phase 1
Tekla 3D Detailing & DSTV Extraction

Detailed connection engineering, clash resolution, and generation of CNC machine code directly from design BIM.

Phase 2
Automated CNC Cutting & Pre-Assembly

Beam sawing, plate burning, bolt-hole drilling, and precision fit-up inside laser-calibrated assembly jigs.

Phase 3
Certified Welding & NDT Quality Inspection

Welding by AWS D1.1 certified personnel followed by 100% Ultrasonic (UT) and Magnetic Particle (MPI) testing.

Phase 4
Blasting, Protective Coating & Phased Delivery

Centrifugal shot blasting, primer/topcoat application, barcode tagging, and sequenced site transport.
