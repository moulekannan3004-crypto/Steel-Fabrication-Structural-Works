FABRICATION FACILITY & WORKMANSHIP FAQS

Common questions regarding our Houston manufacturing plant, visual quality standards, surface coating certifications, and client facility audits.

Yes. We welcome pre-award capability audits, client QA representative walkthroughs, and third-party inspection agencies at our 185,000 sq. ft. Houston facility. Guided walkthroughs allow engineering teams to inspect our CNC drill lines, automated beam welders, blast bays, and active assembly operations first-hand. Contact our technical team to schedule an escorted tour.

Our dedicated automated blast bay performs abrasive cleaning to SSPC-SP 10 / NACE No. 2 (Near-White Blast Cleaning). We apply multi-tier coating systems, including zinc-rich epoxy primers, high-build intermediate epoxies, and UV-resistant polyurethane topcoats, as well as ASTM A123 compliant hot-dip galvanizing for marine and corrosive industrial environments.

Architectural projects featured in our gallery meet AISC Category AESS 1 through AESS 4 standards. For high-visibility applications—such as public atriums, spiral stairs, and canopy trusses—we provide seamless ground-flush welds, hidden backing bars, contoured coping cuts, and ultra-smooth surface sanding to ensure aesthetic appeal.
