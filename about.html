Our Story

BUILT ON INTEGRITY, ADVANCED ENGINEERING & RELENTLESS CRAFTSMANSHIP

Founded in Houston, Texas in 1989, SteelCraft started as a specialized heavy plate welding shop. Over the past 35+ years, we have evolved into one of North America's premier AISC-certified steel fabricators and turnkey structural contractors.

Today, our facility spans 180,000 square feet of heavy fabrication bays equipped with automated 3-spindle CNC beam lines, 1000-ton hydraulic brake presses, robotic plasma cutters, and dedicated submerged arc welding stations. We partner directly with leading EPC contractors, real estate developers, and civil agencies.

Our Mission

To engineer and fabricate world-class structural steel solutions that empower commercial, industrial, and civil infrastructure, ensuring uncompromised structural integrity, zero-defect quality, and timely project delivery.

Our Vision

To be the benchmark of automated structural steel manufacturing, pioneering digital BIM integration, robotic welding precision, and environmentally conscious fabrication across global markets.
