ENGINEERING STRUCTURAL EXCELLENCE FOR OVER THREE DECADES

SteelCraft is a premier structural steel fabrication and erection contractor. Operating from a modernized 180,000 sq. ft. fabrication facility, we specialize in high-complexity structural steel frameworks, pre-engineered metal buildings, heavy plate girders, and turnkey industrial installations.

Combining 3D BIM detailing in Tekla Structures with advanced CNC automated beam lines, submerged arc welding, and certified NDT quality control, we ensure every joint, weld, and bolt conforms to stringent international building codes.

WORLD-CLASS PLANT EQUIPMENT & CAPACITY

Our state-of-the-art Houston manufacturing facility utilizes modern European and American automated CNC equipment to maximize output, reduce fabrication cycle times, and achieve microscopic tolerances.

CNC Beam Line

High-speed 3-spindle drilling, coping, and automated robotic plasma marking.

1000T Brake Press

Extreme-tonnage hydraulic bending for structural cones, transition sections, and thick plates.

Continuous centrifugal shot blaster providing SA 2.5 surface profile with epoxy paint bays.
