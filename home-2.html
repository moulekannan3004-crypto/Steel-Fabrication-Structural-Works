THE 6-STAGE FABRICATION & ERECTION PROCESS

Our rigorous quality gate workflow ensures zero defects from initial CAD import to final bolt torque verification.

01

BIM Detailing & DSTV Extraction

Converting engineer-of-record design drawings into high-fidelity 3D models with individual piece markings and CNC coordinates.

02

Automated CNC Cutting & Coping

Automated beam sawing, robotic plasma beveling, high-def plate burning, and computerized multi-spindle bolt-hole drilling.

03

Precision Fit-Up & Welding

Assembly in laser-calibrated jigs followed by certified Submerged Arc (SAW) and flux-cored welding conforming to AWS D1.1.

04

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

Level II CWI inspection utilizing Ultrasonic (UT) and Magnetic Particle (MPI) examination with digital QA traceability certs.

05

Blasting & Protective Coating

Centrifugal wheel blasting to SSPC-SP 10 (Near-White Metal) followed by high-build epoxy, zinc-rich primers, or galvanizing.

06

Phased Logistics & Erection

Sequenced flatbed delivery matched to site hook-time, supported by certified mobile crane operators and master ironworkers.
