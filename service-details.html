HEAVY STRUCTURAL STEEL MANUFACTURING & SYSTEM DETAILING

SteelCraft is an industry leader in heavy structural steel fabrication. We manufacture complex steel frameworks, wide-flange built-up columns, transfer girders, and seismic moment frames for commercial skyscrapers, industrial processing plants, and critical infrastructure projects.

By leveraging parametric 3D modeling in Tekla Structures, every member is detailed with millimeter-accurate bolt-hole coordinates, bevels, and connection plates. These digital models feed directly into our CNC drilling lines and automated plasma cutters, eradicating manual layout discrepancies and ensuring 100% clash-free jobsite fit-up.

Core Technical Capabilities

Built-Up Box & Plate Girders

Full-penetration Submerged Arc Welded (SAW) box columns and deep plate girders up to 4.5m web depths.

Automated CNC Beam Processing

High-speed 3-spindle carbide drilling, multi-axis robotic plasma coping, and automated piece marking.

Heavy Lifting Capacity

Multiple 65-metric ton overhead bridge cranes capable of handling tandem lifts for massive modular assemblies.

In-House Surface Treatment

Automated centrifugal wheel shot blasting (SSPC-SP 10) with climate-controlled epoxy coating bays.

Fabrication Work Process

Phase 1
Tekla 3D Detailing & DSTV Extraction

Detailed connection engineering, clash resolution, and generation of CNC machine code directly from design BIM.

Phase 2
Automated CNC Cutting & Pre-Assembly

Beam sawing, plate burning, bolt-hole drilling, and precision fit-up inside laser-calibrated assembly jigs.

Phase 3
Certified Welding & NDT Quality Inspection

Welding by AWS D1.1 certified personnel followed by 100% Ultrasonic (UT) and Magnetic Particle (MPI) testing.

Phase 4
Blasting, Protective Coating & Phased Delivery

Centrifugal shot blasting, primer/topcoat application, barcode tagging, and sequenced site transport.
