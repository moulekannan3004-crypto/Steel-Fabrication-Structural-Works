FREQUENTLY ASKED PROJECT & ERECTION QUESTIONS

Detailed answers regarding our structural contracting scope, project management, monthly fabrication capacities, and certified on-site erection coordination.

SteelCraft operates flexibly across several major contracting frameworks, including Design-Assist (working early with structural engineers of record), Turnkey Design-Build, Subcontractor to General Contractors (GCs), and Lump-Sum EPC structural steel packages. We also provide hybrid material-supply and field-erection subcontracts tailored to commercial, industrial, and infrastructure requirements.

Our 185,000 sq. ft. Houston facility features a steady-state monthly output capacity of 3,500+ metric tons (MT) of heavy structural steel. Equipped with twin 40-ton overhead gantry cranes, we can fabricate, handle, and pre-assemble single-span trusses and box girders up to 120 feet in continuous length and up to 80 tons per single lift.

All on-site steel erection is executed by OSHA 30-certified superintendents and AWS-certified field welders in accordance with AISC Code of Standard Practice (AISC 303). We utilize 4D BIM schedule integration and real-time piece-mark barcoding so site managers can track every column, beam, and brace from shop loading to final crane pick and torque inspection.

Our specialized logistics department manages complete multimodal transport, including state DOT wide-load permits, highway escort vehicles, and route clearance surveys for oversized long-span bridge girders and industrial modules. We ensure timely Just-In-Time (JIT) deliveries directly synchronized with site crane availability.

Upon project substantial completion, clients receive a comprehensive SteelCraft Closeout Binder containing certified Mill Test Reports (MTRs), ASNT Level II NDT inspection certificates, bolt torque calibration logs, coating DFT thickness reports, as-built CAD/Tekla models, and our standard 10-to-25-year structural fabrication warranty.
